The service

iAstroGuru offers automated Vedic and Western astrology, Combined chart perspectives, Chinese zodiac, numerology and tarot tools and reports in English and Hindi. There are no human astrologers or live consultations. Your first kundli reading is free after sign-in; free calculator tools use calculations and rules, never AI.

Kundli readings and paid reports are calculated by the advanced iAstroGuru Engine and written either by the iAstroGuru Engine or with the help of AI when enabled; every reading passes multi-layer quality assurance before you see it. Reports written without AI help are not described as AI-written. Read about AI data use.

Adult accounts & children’s details

Accounts are for adults 18+ and you must confirm your age. Add another person’s details only with permission. Only a parent or legal guardian may provide details for a child under 18; they must give explicit consent and their own birth year indicating an age of at least 18. Withdraw consent by deleting the child’s profile. Children are not marketed to, tracked, behaviourally monitored or targeted with advertising.

Give accurate birth details and mark uncertain birth times honestly. Incorrect time, place or time zone can change the result. Protect your account and sign-in codes. Ask questions & payment

Ask iAstroGuru is a paid AI service, paid per question. A free check before payment shows the question's level and price: one focused question (a 120–250-word answer) or two linked topics or detailed timing (300–450 words).
