An interpretive tradition

Vedic and Western astrology, Chinese zodiac, numerology and tarot are cultural and interpretive traditions. Our charts use astronomical calculations, but their interpretations are not scientifically established predictions. Readings are for reflection, not prediction.

Not professional advice

No report or tool provides medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. We do not diagnose illness, prescribe treatment, direct investments or determine legal outcomes. Base health, mental health, legal and money decisions on qualified advice and real circumstances. Contact local emergency services if you are in immediate danger.

Without fear or guarantees

We do not predict death or illness. Marriage, children, wealth, jobs, compatibility and other life outcomes are not guaranteed. Doshas, gemstones and remedies are traditional descriptions, not curses or required treatments. We do not sell remedies, rituals or gemstones or pressure you to buy through fear. Do not stop treatment or sacrifice essential spending for a traditional practice.

Calculation & writing limits

The service is automated; there are no human astrologers. Incorrect birth time, coordinates, time zone or uncertain historical records can change results. Traditions and ayanamsha choices also differ. Free tools never use AI. Kundli readings and paid reports may be written by the iAstroGuru Engine or with the help of AI when enabled; multi-layer quality checks reduce the risk of error but cannot eliminate it.
