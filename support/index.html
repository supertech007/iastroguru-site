Sign in & your first reading

Sign in. Google supplies your name and verified email; when phone sign-in is enabled, Supabase sends and verifies an SMS code. Your first kundli reading is free, with no card required.

An unavailable sign-in option has not been activated yet. Local development preview may use a shared test account; use fictional details there. Accounts are for adults; add other people’s data only with permission.

Free tools & report writing

Vedic and Western charts, Chinese zodiac, numerology, tarot and other free tools use calculations and rules, never AI. Details carried into the saved-profile form may be held temporarily in the same tab. Kundli reading and paid report writing uses the engine or enabled AI according to the owner setting. Read about data use.

Find, correct or remove readings

Open My readings with the same account. To correct birth details, choose “Use these details” and create a new reading. The earlier report does not change automatically. Use privacy controls to export or remove reading data; export first if you want a copy.

A page failing to load does not mean records were deleted; refresh when your connection returns. Reading-data removal is not account closure or erasure of payment and consent records. Contact support for other privacy requests.

Language, theme & PDF

Use the header to change English/Hindi, dark/light theme and motion preferences. Use the available report download controls for colour or black-and-white PDFs. These files may contain private birth details; ask the person’s permission before sharing.

Payment or missing report help

Paid purchases remain closed until launch; samples are in the report catalogue. Once purchases open, do not pay again if your bank shows a debit without confirmation. Send your order ID and payment reference to support@iastroguru.com.

Reports appear in your account. At launch, automatic report emails will come from guru@iastroguru.com. Read the delivery and refund policies.

Need more help?

Write to support@iastroguru.com. We aim to reply within two working days, acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within one month. Never send passwords, one-time codes or card details. See contact & Grievance Officer details.
