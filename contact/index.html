Our company

iAstroGuru is a brand of Shiv Shakti Software Solutions, a Udyam-registered sole proprietorship of Mohan Minda, New Delhi.

Customer Care

For support, cancellations, refunds and privacy requests, write to support@iastroguru.com. This is the planned Customer Care inbox on Zoho Mail; domain email activates at launch.

For payment questions, include your order ID and payment reference. For data rights, include your account email or phone number. Do not send card numbers, banking passwords, one-time codes or unnecessary birth details. We may ask for proportionate account verification through a secure process.

Reports & automatic email

At launch, reports, receipts and automatic customer emails will come from guru@iastroguru.com, with replies going to support@iastroguru.com. Automatic sending is not active yet. Marketing requires a separate opt-in and an unsubscribe option.

Response & complaint timelines

We aim to answer support requests within two working days. We will acknowledge consumer complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within one month. Privacy requests are handled within applicable legal deadlines. Put “Security” in the subject when reporting a security concern or data breach.
